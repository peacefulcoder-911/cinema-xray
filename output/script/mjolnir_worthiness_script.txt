In Age of Ultron, when Steve Rogers barely nudged Thor's hammer, Thor's smile instantly froze in sheer panic. Did Cap suddenly become worthy in Endgame, or did he fake it five years earlier? The answer reveals Steve's true character. Odin's enchantment states that whoever holds the hammer, if he be worthy, possesses the power of Thor. There is no such thing as being 'a little bit worthy'. You can either lift it, or you cannot. The second Steve felt Mjolnir budge, his humility kicked in. He stopped pulling to spare Thor's ego in front of the team. Five years later, with Thanos crushing Thor's chest, politeness was off the table. When Thor screams 'I knew it!', he wasn't surprised Steve was worthy. He knew Cap had lied to protect his feelings all along.
