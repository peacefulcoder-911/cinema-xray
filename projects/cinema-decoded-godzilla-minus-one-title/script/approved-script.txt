Have you ever wondered why the movie is called Godzilla Minus One?

Here's the reason.

The story takes place in Japan just after World War II.

The war has already left the country devastated. Its cities are destroyed, its economy is broken, and people are struggling to rebuild their lives.

In other words, Japan is already at zero.

Then Godzilla appears.

And instead of letting the country recover, he causes even more destruction.

Japan goes from zero to minus one.

That's where the title comes from.

Godzilla doesn't just destroy a country.

He pushes a country that has already lost everything even further into disaster.
