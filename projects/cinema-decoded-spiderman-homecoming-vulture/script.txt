You never asked why a New York salvage crew was holding alien weapons, and the answer is one cancelled contract.

Spider-Man: Homecoming shows you this in its opening minutes and never says it out loud.

After the Battle of New York, Adrian Toomes runs a salvage company hired to clean up the wreckage. Real crew, real payroll.

Then Damage Control, a government outfit with Stark's backing, takes the job over.

Toomes is left with a warehouse of alien tech and a crew that still needs paying. So he keeps the tech and builds weapons from it.

That's the answer: one lost contract. The Vulture isn't chasing power, he's protecting a payroll.
