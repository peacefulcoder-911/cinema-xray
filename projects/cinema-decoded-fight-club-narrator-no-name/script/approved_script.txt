Notice how in Fight Club, Edward Norton’s character never actually has a name? Throughout the entire movie, nobody ever calls him by his real name. In the script, he’s simply credited as "The Narrator." Now, a lot of people think his name is Jack because he keeps saying lines like: "I am Jack’s smirking revenge." "I am Jack’s cold sweat." But Jack isn’t his name at all. He’s just reading old magazine articles written from the perspective of human organs. So why did David Fincher refuse to give him a name? Because his character has completely erased his own identity. He’s an anonymous corporate drone who defined his entire existence through IKEA catalogs, coffee tables, and consumer brands. He felt so completely invisible that his mind literally split in two to survive. The Narrator doesn't have a name because he gave all his confidence, courage, and freedom to someone else: Tyler Durden.
