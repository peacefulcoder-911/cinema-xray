You never asked why Peter smiles as a single man, and the answer is pure psychological horror. Look closely at his solitary apartment: rent notices piling up, a mattress on the floor, and a lingering phantom ache he cannot name. He thinks this grueling isolation is the price of keeping loved ones safe from the shadows. But notice what happens when he crosses paths with Mary Jane Watson. There is no shared history, yet their eyes lock with an unbearable, suffocating weight. The universe erased their marriage, but it could not purge the muscle memory of devotion. He believes he chose his freedom. In reality, he is living inside an erased life, smiling through a quiet, invisible lobotomy where every victory tastes like ash.
