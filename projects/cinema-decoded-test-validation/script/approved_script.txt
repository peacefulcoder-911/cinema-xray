Why does everyone stare at the spinning top at the end of Inception, when it was never Cobb's real totem?
The top belonged to Mal. Cobb took it after she died. But a totem's single rule is that nobody else can ever touch it.
Instead, look closely at Dom Cobb's left hand. In the real world, Cobb wears no wedding ring. But whenever he enters a dream, the gold wedding band suddenly reappears on his finger.
In the final airport scene, Cobb's left hand is completely bare. He is truly awake.
The spinning top was just Nolan's greatest misdirection.
