GTA 6 doesn't give you just one criminal to follow.

It gives you two—and they're nothing alike.

Meet Jason Duval.

He grew up around criminals, tried to leave that life behind by joining the Army, but eventually ended up working for local drug runners in the Florida Keys.

Now meet Lucia Caminos.

She grew up learning to fight, and her loyalty to her family eventually landed her in prison.

But here's where things get interesting.

Jason wants an easier life. Lucia wants a better one, and she's willing to take risks to get it.

Then an easy score goes wrong.

And suddenly, they're caught in a criminal conspiracy stretching across the entire state of Leonida.

Now they have to rely on each other to survive.

So GTA 6 isn't just about two criminals.

It's about two people whose biggest strength—and possibly their biggest weakness—is each other.
