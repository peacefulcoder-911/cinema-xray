# Grand Theft Auto VI — Character Research & Fact-Check Dossier

## 1. Character Profiles

### Jason Duval
* **Background & Early Life:** Grew up in Florida / Leonida surrounded by grifters, petty crooks, and criminal associates.
* **Military Service:** Attempted to break away from the cycle of crime by enlisting in the U.S. Army.
* **Return to Crime:** After military discharge, struggled with civilian life and ended up working for local low-level drug runners operating out of the Florida Keys / Leonida coastal waterways.
* **Core Motivation:** Wants an *easier life*—avoiding unnecessary bloodshed and looking for a way out of grinding survival.
* **Relationship to Lucia:** Partners in crime and romance; provides tactical awareness, driving, and firepower.

### Lucia Caminos
* **Background & Early Life:** First female protagonist in the modern HD GTA era; raised by a father who taught her how to fight and stand her ground from childhood.
* **Incarceration:** Fierce loyalty to her family led her to take violent action or take the fall, landing her in the Leonida Department of Corrections (seen in prison uniform at the beginning of Trailer 1 with parole officer Stefanie).
* **Core Motivation:** Wants a *better life*—unwilling to remain trapped in poverty or servitude, fiercely determined to take control of her own destiny, and willing to take high-stakes risks to achieve freedom and wealth.
* **Relationship to Jason:** The emotional and strategic catalyst; pushes the duo into higher-stakes scores while forging a deep "us against the world" pact ("Trust? Trust.").

## 2. Narrative Arc & Setting

* **Setting:** The State of Leonida (including Vice City, Port Gellhorn, the Florida Keys, the Everglades/grassrivers, and Lake Leonida).
* **The Incident:** An "easy score" robbery goes catastrophically wrong, triggering attention from federal authorities, rival syndicates, and corrupt officials.
* **The Conspiracy:** Jason and Lucia are drawn into a sprawling criminal conspiracy stretching across the political and cartel landscape of Leonida.
* **Thematic Thesis:** Their relationship is both their ultimate survival tool (strength) and their fatal vulnerability (weakness), creating a modern Bonnie & Clyde archetype.
