You completely missed the most terrifying line in the new Spider-Man trailer. At the thirty-second mark, Peter Parker isn't fighting a villain. Could you get rid of the bad aspects, but keep the good?" Look closely at Banner’s holographic monitor. Flashing in the corner is a medical alert: "Retroviral Acceleration." And in the opening seconds, look at Peter’s forearm: black microscopic veins pulsing beneath his skin. This isn't superhero fatigue. Marvel is adapting the legendary comic storyline where Peter's radioactive spider-bite begins to mutate his cellular biology, slowly destroying his body from the inside out. Peter gave up his identity to save the world. Now, the very power that makes him Spider-Man might be the thing killing him.
