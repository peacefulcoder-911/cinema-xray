Inside the brutal gold mines of KGF, power wasn’t measured in gold—it was measured in pure terror.
Twenty thousand enslaved workers lived in total submission because Garuda held an absolute monopoly on fear. Anyone who looked up was executed.
Every assassin sent to eliminate Garuda made the exact same mistake: they tried to fight him with weapons.
Rocky understood something deeper.
A dictator only rules as long as the oppressed believe defiance is impossible.
So in a world where survival meant dropping to your knees, Rocky refused to bow.
Every strike he delivered wasn't to prove his physical strength—it was to shatter the illusion of Garuda's invincibility.
Rocky didn’t just enter KGF to kill a tyrant. He entered an empire built on fear, and made fear change sides.
