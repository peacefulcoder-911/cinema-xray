There's a small detail in Doctor Strange that reveals how much Stephen Strange lost.

Before his accident, Christine gives Stephen a watch.

But look closely at its engraving.

It says, 'Time will tell how much I love you.'

At first, it seems like a romantic gift.

Then Stephen's accident destroys the life he built around his hands.

His career disappears. His confidence collapses. And his relationship with Christine begins to fall apart.

But he keeps that broken watch.

Even after everything changes, he refuses to let it go.

The watch becomes a reminder of the person he was, the person he lost, and the love he couldn't hold on to.

In a movie about controlling time, the most important thing Stephen cannot repair is his own past.
