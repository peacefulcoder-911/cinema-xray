## 1. The Rules of the 50th Hunger Games (Second Quarter Quell)
* **Standard Hunger Games Protocol:**
  - 12 Districts of Panem.
  - 2 Tributes selected per district (1 boy, 1 girl) via the annual Reaping.
  - Standard Total: $12 \times 2 = 24$ Tributes.
  - Number of opponents any single tribute faces: **23 opponents**.
* **The Second Quarter Quell Twist:**
  - Occurred on the 50th anniversary of the Treaty of Treason.
  - As a reminder that two rebels died for each Capitol citizen during the First Rebellion, the Capitol doubled the harvest:
  - 4 Tributes demanded per district (**2 boys and 2 girls**).
  - Quell Total: $12 \times 4 = 48$ Children entering the arena.
  - Number of opponents Haymitch faces: **47 opponents**.

## 2. Haymitch Abernathy's Story
* **Origin:** District 12 (the Seam / coal mining district).
* **Timeline:** 24 years prior to Katniss Everdeen and Peeta Mellark entering the 74th Hunger Games.
* **The Arena:** Designed as a deceptive paradise with toxic vegetation, volcanic geysers, poisonous carnivores, and aggressive muttations.
* **Survival & Victory:** Haymitch outsmarted the Capitol by using the arena's force field physics (rebounding an axe throw), an act of defiance that infuriated President Coriolanus Snow.
