Imagine being trapped with a creature that looks exactly like your friends. Now imagine having no way to know who is human.

Inside Outpost 31, an alien organism replicates living tissue with microscopic perfection. The danger isn't in the storm outside—it's sitting in the room with you.

This destroys the very foundation of survival: trust. Every nervous glance and defensive answer could be ordinary fear—or a monster wearing a human face.

To survive, MacReady ties his crew down and plunges a glowing hot wire into their blood, forcing the hidden organism to violently reveal itself.

The test works. But humanity has been reduced to a chemical reaction, where innocent men must prove they are human before anyone will trust them.

The terror isn't just that the alien can imitate anyone. It's that once trust dies, everyone looks like a monster.
