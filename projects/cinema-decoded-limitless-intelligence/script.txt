What if becoming smarter actually made your life more dangerous?
Eddie Morra begins as an unmotivated writer struggling with direction and creative paralysis.
Then, a single pill of NZT-48 changes everything.
Suddenly, his mind operates at total cognitive clarity.
He finishes his book in days, masters foreign languages, and turns thousands into millions on Wall Street.
Yet as his ambition grows, a critical contradiction emerges.
His abilities expand far faster than his ability to manage the consequences.
Eddie can outthink financial titans, yet he borrows cash from a violent loan shark.
He sees endless possibilities, yet his survival becomes trapped by chemical dependence.
Being able to see more options doesn't automatically mean knowing which ones are worth choosing.
NZT gives Eddie a more powerful mind. But it doesn't automatically give him a better understanding of what to do with it.
Because intelligence can help you get what you want. Wisdom helps you decide whether you should want it.
