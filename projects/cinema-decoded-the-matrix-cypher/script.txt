Cypher knows the steak is fake. So why does he want another bite?
He knows the entire world around him is an illusion. He's already escaped it. But sitting across from Agent Smith, he makes a decision.
He looks at the steak, tastes it, and admits he knows it isn't real. Then he says something terrifying: ignorance is bliss.
Cypher isn't asking whether the Matrix is real. He already knows the answer. He's asking whether the truth is worth the suffering that comes with it.
Because sometimes, the hardest part of knowing the truth... is accepting that you still want the lie.
