## 2. Distinction: Confirmed Facts vs. Unconfirmed Theories

### ✅ CONFIRMED FACTS
1. **Incursion Mechanics**: The boundary erosion between realities destroys one or both colliding universes completely.
2. **Stephen Strange's Past Incursion**: Doctor Strange dreamwalking with the Darkhold caused the annihilation of an entire parallel reality (*Multiverse of Madness*).
3. **Clea's Mission**: Earth-616 Doctor Strange was recruited at the conclusion of *Multiverse of Madness* to resolve an active incursion threatening the cosmos.
4. **Avengers: Doomsday Production**: Directed by Anthony & Joe Russo, starring Robert Downey Jr. as Victor von Doom, premiering May 2026.

### ⚠️ UNCONFIRMED / SPECULATIVE CLAIMS (STRICTLY GUARDED IN SCRIPT)
1. **Specific Earths Colliding in Doomsday**: Which specific cinematic realities (e.g. Fox X-Men Universe Earth-10005 vs. MCU Earth-616) will collide has NOT been officially confirmed by Marvel Studios.
2. **Doom's Exact Role in the Incursion**: Whether Victor von Doom causes the incursion, stops it, or weaponizes it into Battleworld remains unconfirmed in official marketing.
3. **Trailer Claims**: Promotional teasers released in late 2026 feature cosmic rifts and multiverse concepts, but specific battle details remain under strict Marvel Studios NDA. The narration script adheres strictly to confirmed incursion lore without fabricating unreleased plot points.
