# Research References: Marvel Multiverse Incursions

## 1. Cinematic Source References (MCU Canonical)

### *Doctor Strange in the Multiverse of Madness* (2022)
- **Scene**: Earth-838 Illuminati Chamber & Destroyed Sinister Strange Universe.
- **Key Dialogue (Reed Richards / Mister Fantastic)**:
  > *"An incursion occurs when the boundary between two universes erodes and they collide, destroying one or both entirely."*
- **Key Dialogue (Professor Charles Xavier & Illuminati)**:
  > *"Stephen Strange used the Darkhold to dreamwalk into other realities to find a solution to Thanos... In doing so, he triggered an incursion that wiped out an entire universe."*
- **Visual Evidence**:
  - Desolate Earth with crumbling gravitational geometry and shattered reality where Sinister Strange lives.
  - Mid-credits scene: Clea (Charlize Theron) confronts Doctor Strange in New York: *"You caused an incursion, and we're gonna fix it. Unless you're afraid?"* — leading directly into a rift in the Dark Dimension.

### *Loki* (Seasons 1 & 2)
- **Concept**: Multiverse branching, temporal loom overload, and catastrophic collapse of colliding timelines when branched realities overlap without restriction.
- **He Who Remains**: Explains that without pruning or controlling the branches, an infinite multiversal war occurs where variants battle for the survival of their respective universes.

## 2. Comic Book Canonical Precedent (Jonathan Hickman's Marvel Run)

### *New Avengers* (Vol. 3, 2013–2015) & *Infinity* (2013)
- **Writer**: Jonathan Hickman.
- **The Mechanic**:
  - The contraction of the Multiverse causes the focal point of two colliding universes to be their respective Earths.
  - An incursion event lasts precisely **8 hours**.
  - If both Earths remain in the collision space at the expiration of the timer, **both entire universes are permanently annihilated**.
  - If one Earth is destroyed before the timer expires, the collision is averted, and **both remaining universes survive** (at the cost of the destroyed planet).

### *Time Runs Out* & *Secret Wars* (2015)
- **The Moral Catastrophe**:
  - The Illuminati (Iron Man, Mister Fantastic, Black Panther, Namor, Beast, Black Bolt) build planet-killing weapons (Antimatter Injectors).
  - The central philosophical dilemma: *Can heroes commit genocide of a parallel Earth to save their own universe?*
  - The final incursion occurs between Earth-616 (Main Marvel Universe) and Earth-1610 (Ultimate Marvel Universe), culminating in the destruction of the multiverse and the formation of Battleworld by Doctor Doom.

## 3. Official *Avengers: Doomsday* (2026) Promotional & Production Facts

### Confirmed Production Facts (Marvel Studios / San Diego Comic-Con 2024)
- **Title**: *Avengers: Doomsday* (formerly *Avengers: The Kang Dynasty*).
- **Release Date**: May 1, 2026.
- **Directors**: Anthony and Joe Russo (Russo Brothers).
- **Screenplay**: Stephen McFeely.
- **Lead Antagonist**: Robert Downey Jr. cast as Victor von Doom / Doctor Doom.
- **Sequential Follow-up**: *Avengers: Secret Wars* (May 7, 2027).

### Promoted Narrative & Multiverse Saga Context
- Marvel Studios has explicitly confirmed that the Multiverse Saga culminates in *Avengers: Doomsday* and *Avengers: Secret Wars*.
- The incursion mechanic established in *Multiverse of Madness* serves as the fundamental existential threat driving the conflict between disparate Marvel realities.
