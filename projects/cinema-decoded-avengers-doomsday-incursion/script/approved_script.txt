What if saving your universe meant destroying another one?

In Marvel, this nightmare has a name.

An incursion.

It happens when two separate universes begin colliding.

And here's the terrifying part.

Both universes cannot survive the collision.

One or both must be destroyed.

Think about what that means.

Your heroes could save billions of people in their universe...

Only by allowing billions of people somewhere else to die.

Suddenly, the biggest threat isn't a villain trying to conquer the universe.

It's a choice where every possible victory comes with an unimaginable loss.

And that's why the multiverse could become Marvel's most dangerous battlefield.
