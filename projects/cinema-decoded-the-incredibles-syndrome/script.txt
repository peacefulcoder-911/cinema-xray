Syndrome says nobody should be special. But his entire life is spent trying to prove that he is.

As a boy named Buddy Pine, he idolized Mr. Incredible, begging to be his sidekick. But when he was rejected and told he wasn't special, that humiliation became his identity.

Without natural powers, Buddy used technology to reinvent himself as Syndrome. He built zero-point energy and the lethal Omnidroid—not to stand beside superheroes, but to eliminate them.

His famous philosophy claimed: "When everyone's super, no one will be."

Yet look at what he actually did. He staged a catastrophic attack on the city just to swoop in and play the savior. He wasn't abolishing heroes. He was engineering a world where he was the only hero left to worship.

Syndrome doesn't want a world without heroes. He wants a world where everyone finally sees him as one.
