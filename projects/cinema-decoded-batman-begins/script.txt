Notice how Batman was not built on fearlessness, but on the very terror that broke Bruce Wayne. As a boy, Bruce plunged into a collapsed well where a shrieking swarm of bats scarred his mind. His father taught him that falling is how we learn to rise, but the trauma remained trapped in the darkness. Years later, Henri Ducard forces him to face the truth: you cannot destroy fear. To master your enemy, you have to become it. Deep inside the cave, Bruce steps directly into the bat swarm. He stops running. He rises with them. When Alfred asks why bats, Bruce gives the chilling answer: Bats frighten me. It's time my enemies shared my dread. He didn't eliminate his childhood weakness. He inverted it into his armor, his weapon, and his permanent identity.
